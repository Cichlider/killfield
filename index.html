killfield

A search-based agent for a maze tank duel. Most of its kills are bank shots.

 

Arrow keys or ESDF to drive · M / Space / Q to fire · R for a new maze

Bullets ricochet and stay lethal for ten seconds — including to whoever fired them. A round is not decided the instant someone dies: the world keeps running for three more seconds, so a shot already in the air can still take the apparent winner with it.
